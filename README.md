# Finance-Executive-Summary-Dashboard
The finance team needed a consolidated view of profit trends, geographical distribution of sales and product segment performance to identify revenue concentration risks across different business units. 

Tools used:
Power BI Desktop

DAX (Creating custom KPI cards and dynamic time-intelligence measures).

Key Insight #1: Identified that France, Germany and Canada accounted for approximately 66% of total sales, highlighting a significant revenue concentration risk.

Key Insight #2: Tracked monthly profit trends to identify peak performance in December 2014.
